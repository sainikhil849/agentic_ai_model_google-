About
Let's Escape Room offers a thrilling escape from daily life, inviting you to solve clues, puzzles, games, and encoded challenges with your team or as a solo player. The theme will be briefed to you at the venue. This clock-driven challenge requires you to escape from a theme-based room using logic, hints, and teamwork to find your path to freedom. Easier said than done; everything you need to escape is inside the room, waiting to meet your eyes. Analyse, interpret, and conclude to progress. The concept is unique, thrilling, and safe, with no horror elements. It requires no physical exertion and is suitable for corporate teams, couples, friends, and families. A free voucher will be awarded on game completion. Note: We have no branches. Multiple time slots are available throughout the day.
